More Than Just a Ride — It’s Your Personal Hampi Story

Hampi’s monuments are spread across 26 sq. km of dramatic boulder valleys. Exploring via our dedicated, open-air private auto-rickshaw (tuk-tuk) gives you panoramic 360° views, effortless temple-to-temple access, and zero parking hassle.

Hampi Travel Tips & FAQs

Each auto comfortably accommodates up to 3 adult passengers (or 2 adults + 2 small children). If you are a group of 4 to 12 travelers, we arrange multiple Orange Autos traveling together as a coordinated convoy!
The peak pleasant season is October to March, though monsoon (July–September) makes the boulders lush green. For daily sightseeing, starting early around 6:00 AM – 8:00 AM lets you enjoy cool weather, soft golden light, and smaller crowds at Vittala and Virupaksha Temples.
Yes! Hosapete Junction (HPT) is just 13 km (about 25 minutes) from Hampi Bazaar. We offer direct station/bus-stand pickups, luggage drop-off at your homestay/hotel, and immediate start to your sightseeing tour.
Wear comfortable walking shoes or easy slip-on sandals (since shoes must be removed outside active temples), modest clothing covering shoulders and knees for temple entry, sunglasses, a sun hat, sunscreen, and a refillable water bottle.
