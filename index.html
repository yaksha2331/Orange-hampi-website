Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking an auto tour in Hampi.

How do I book a tour with Orange Hampi?

You can instantly book by clicking any WhatsApp button on this website, filling out the booking assistant form, or messaging directly at +91 82772 66756.

Where do the tours start and end?

I offer convenient pickup and drop-off directly from your hotel/resort in Hampi or Hosapete, as well as Hosapete Railway Station (HPT).

Does the driver act as a tour guide?

Yes! As your driver, I handle all transport, smart route planning, timing, and photo spots. For deep historical insights inside major temples, licensed ASI monument guides can also be arranged on request.

How many people can fit in an auto-rickshaw?

A comfortable auto-rickshaw easily accommodates up to 3 adults. For larger families or groups, I can arrange multiple private auto-rickshaws to travel together.
